Neural 2.0 runs a crew of AI models on your own PC. A director plans the edit. The other stations scout, build, review and render it inside your own After Effects through one guarded native lane. You only step in to accept or give notes.

Set it upSee how it works
The crew

Five stations around one director.

Each station is a seat you fill with a model from your own subscriptions. In the scene, they orbit the director, which owns every creative decision.

Director
Owns the brief, the creative plan and the final calls. Use your strongest model here: Opus, GPT Sol or Gemini high.
Plan
Scouts the footage, music and references, then reviews the director's plan before anything is built.
Edit
Builds the prototype, then the full composition, in After Effects through TonyFX. It also applies the repairs.
Review
A harsh frame-by-frame pass, then a Goldfish pass from a reviewer with no prior context.
Deliver
Renders, exports and hands the cut to you for screening.

Relay: local Qwen assistant on your CPU · Screening: watch and add timestamped notes · Debug: health and locks · Manual Edit: human only, no AI ever acts here

One revision

Ten phases. Reviews can send it back.

The frames spiralling around the core are one revision moving through the pipeline. Any review can send the work to repair, which loops back to Edit. Nothing ships until you accept it.

Routing and events

Cheap seats first. No polling.

Every station has an ordered list of seats. When a seat is unavailable or out of quota, the gateway moves to the next one at a safe boundary. It never moves in the middle of an AE command, and it never adds a paid API.

  1. 1Free OpenCode models worker default
  2. 2Claude Sonnet
  3. 3Claude Haiku
  4. 4Codex Terra / Luna last

Reserved directors are opus, gpt-6.1-sol, gpt-6-sol and astra. Stations wait on real completion events instead of polling, so idle time costs no tokens:

# blocks until a result exists, then acknowledge after reading it
python station_cli.py wait --recipient Director
The native lane

One writer into After Effects.

The cyan beam in the scene is the only path into AE. The station holding the lease writes a command. The TonyFX panel runs it inside After Effects and writes back a signed result. If a result is missing, the lane inspects AE. It never sends the command again.

Documents/ae-mcp-bridge/ae_command.json
{ "id": "cmd_0412", "op": "add_layer", "comp": "MAIN_1080x1920" }

ae_mcp_result.json
{ "id": "cmd_0412", "ok": true, "_servicedBy": "TonyFX" }

The build stays editable: labelled precomps, real keyframes and native effects. You can take over any layer by hand.

House rules

What keeps the edits clean.

  • Never replay UNKNOWN.An unconfirmed AE command is inspected, never re-sent.
  • Beats before effects.Story-first footage flow, with cuts locked to the music and ramps only on impact moments.
  • Fresh eyes once.One Goldfish reviewer with no context, at most one targeted recheck. No endless loops.
  • Substitute, never fake.If a plugin or font is missing, the plan says so and uses a native substitute.
  • Only you accept.Tests, sampled frames and listening are gates. Acceptance is human.
Get started

Clone, set up, sign in.

You need Windows, Python 3.12+, After Effects and at least one AI CLI. Setup downloads the Electron runtime, installs the TonyFX panel and adds a desktop shortcut. Add -Voice for the local voice and dictation models.
