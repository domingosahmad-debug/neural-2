# Action ownership and expressive typography — human review correction

Trigger: V9 LOVE ME full review B. Human rejected incoherent technique setup/payoff, interrupted Tanjiro technique, and generic text that ignores fire/blue-technique semantics.

Failure: adding an authentic speaking performance as a top picture layer can hide the rapid action, decisive strike or transition underneath. A complete audio/text phrase is not a coherent action sequence. Likewise, a layer named after an attack does not prove a visible payoff; a text ledger missing source detail does not prove an attack is absent. Sampled V9 actually shows a flame arc around24s, contradicting an overstrong text-only diagnosis.

Required method: map the final visible picture owners, not only every enabled layer. Bind a whole source-matched quote to a motivated place in anticipation → technique → contact/follow-through → reaction → consequence. Review what the viewer really sees at each boundary. If a quote destroys action rhythm, move the complete event coherently or omit it; never stack replacements over the payoff or truncate speech/action to fit. Distinguish legitimate repeated technique-introduction words in separate stages from replaying the same source event.

Type must express the actual technique through letterform, reveal, material, restrained effect and scene integration. Fire and blue compass geometry need different design systems. One condensed font, lower-third position and color tweak is not sufficient. Exact wording, readable complete glyphs, source match and editability still apply. A typeproof is not approval of final motion/audio/sequence.
