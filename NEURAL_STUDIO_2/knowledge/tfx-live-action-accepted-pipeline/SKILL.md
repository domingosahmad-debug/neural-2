# Accepted live-action character editing

Tony accepted all five completed Dexter edits on4November2026 as the usual film/TV TikTok standard. Chemical F03 and Offline F04 established the method; Taxidermy, Sweet Daydream and Pitiful extend the accepted references. New instructions may add treatments; otherwise preserve this standard. [Exact accepted versions and measured examples](references/chemical-offline-accepted.md) identify the reference; earlier F01/F02 drafts are not accepted examples. Use this method automatically for comparable live-action character edits. For other real-world formats, adapt the story and tone to the brief; do not impose Dexter's menace or a character montage on a stand-up/event story. Anime retains its separate accepted pipeline.

## Build the edit around the performance and music

1. Establish a strong authentic spoken intro that supplies context. Keep the complete performance and exact words. Remove episode backing score with the verified local dialogue/stem preparation. Use the chosen edit song quietly beneath the speech, with a smooth rise into the body. Reverb is optional and must preserve intelligibility. Keep dialogue, music and motivated SFX separately editable.
2. In the music body, choose compelling character moments: deliberate action, a serious look, an eerie smile, expressive movement and varied useful framing. Connect shots through mood, pose, direction and visual energy. The intro supplies the story; a continuous episode-like action sequence is not required in the body. Avoid interchangeable stares, static filler, unmotivated inserts, weak dark subjects and unsynchronized talking mouths.
3. Map the exact chosen song section's audible beats, accents and transitions before cutting. Place a new scene on each chosen musical cut/major accent, with action or expression matching that emphasis. Do not let strong beats pass arbitrarily or cut every detected micro-onset mechanically. Design shot durations and the ending for that song; about30seconds is the accepted pair's scale, not a padding requirement.
4. Scout real original source ranges and inspect the whole range used by retiming, not one thumbnail. Use existing indexed footage, local selection evidence and measured source handles. Machine tags and face detection assist selection; they do not decide the crop or artistic choice. Use new footage decisions for new edits, never copy a completed accepted scene/timeline as source material.

## Motion and framing

5. Use Tony's isolated Twixtor ownership and exactly three Speed keys150→35→150 with the established smooth U-shaped VALUE curve where that treatment is used. Preserve source FPS, sufficient integrated source consumption/handles and one retime owner. Reject warped hands/faces, frozen poses or crossed source cuts. Strong source motion matters more than applying the effect everywhere.
6. Keep added BlurMoCurves/zoom on a named shot-bounded adjustment above the scene. Put directional blur on a separate upper adjustment. Retiming, source grade and added zoom remain independent. Use the measured authored impact/easing from tfx-motion-transitions, not generic linear drift or alternating canned zooms. Adapt centers to the adjustment's coordinate space and keep readable settles.
7. Center the important face, body, hands or object deliberately. Check crop and readability through entry, movement, zoom extrema and exit. Luma statistics alone cannot prove that a face reads well. Correct a poor crop or choose a stronger source angle before adding effects; do not hide the subject in darkness or crop a head unintentionally.

## Centered kinetic typography

8. Use editable native word-by-word captions aligned to actual spoken onsets and full words. Prepare/verify per-word clocks; do not use evenly spaced guesses. Type stays centered in the middle of the screen, including partial reveals. Do not leave the first revealed word anchored at the left edge of a full-phrase box.
9. Make the animation engaging and coherent: animated scale/position/blur or equivalent authored treatment, then a readable hold and deliberate exit. Simple fade-in/fade-out alone is insufficient. Use a clear supporting/name/payoff/hero hierarchy, restrained tracking on supporting text, larger hero words and contrast against the source. The accepted red hero/cream support palette is a useful reference, not compulsory on every song.
10. Fit the COMPLETE animated reveal, not just its settled rectangle. Stack long hero phrases into centered lines when appropriate. Check every word change and maximum overshoot for clipping and face readability. The pair's tested sizes/overshoots are examples; retune for each phrase, font, composition and background. Avoid random spin, wobble and unreadable flashes.

## Posting captions: stylized censorship

For film/TV TikTok edits, censor visible profanity and the words SERIAL, KILLER, KILLERS and their phrase combinations by default. Use a designed internal asterisk, masking glyph or short graphic interruption (for example S*RIAL K*LLERS, SH*T, F**K), retaining recognizability, exact spoken timing, centered kinetic animation and visual hierarchy. A color/font change alone leaves the word uncensored. Inspect complete and partial reveals so no uncensored source text flashes through. Refit animated bounds after substitution and keep editable layers. Apply consistently across captions and other visible text; retain original transcripts separately. This is Tony's posting preference, not proof of platform reach or immunity from moderation. Do not alter audio or other accepted work unless requested.

## Finish without repeating accepted work

11. Freeze a revision-bound source/music/quote/type/ending blueprint, then use bounded meaningful proof before scaling a materially new treatment. Use actual Goldfish fresh review where it can prevent a costly wrong build; reuse adequate same-scope review and do not create a review loop for mechanical propagation of this accepted method.
12. Preserve good intros/manual work and all accepted pair artifacts. Use new AEP variants for the remaining edits. One Neural native writer, exact live guards and matching results, no UNKNOWN replay. Local tools handle extraction, clocks, source bounds, audio, decode and packaging; models make creative choices from actual evidence.
13. Render at the intended geometry/cadence. Accepted masters are1080square at24000/1001 with separately prepared music/dialogue and about-14LUFS. Preserve clean ProRes/native intermediates and derive review/delivery copies locally. Heavy756frame renders on this host took46minutes: use the corrected60minute native observation/65minute TonyFX wait, cache reuse and actual completion watches. A timeout does not prove a crash or cancellation; inspect exact own reports and full decode before any new operation.
14. Check source/picture, timing/motion, typography, dialogue/music ownership, ending and complete decode separately. Technical checks or stills do not prove playback/listening. Tony's acceptance covers the exact five recorded Dexter versions; future edits still need their own final review. Stop inference during watched local work, and stop verification once sufficient current proof is complete.
