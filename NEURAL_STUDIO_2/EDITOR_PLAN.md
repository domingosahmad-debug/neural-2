## Versioned render contract / review resolutions
neural.edit.v1 uses a contiguous sequential V1 track; no gaps, overlays or transitions. Output is constant 24/25/30/60 fps with even canvas dimensions <=1920. Clip duration is integer output frames (nearest backend, floor in trim UI); source in remains seconds and output sample positions are source-in + frame/fps. Source video fits inside the canvas with black padding, no crop. Rotation follows FFmpeg/HTML decoder metadata. Variable-rate footage is normalized to project fps. PCM intermediate audio avoids accumulating AAC encoder delay at cuts; final AAC is 48 kHz stereo. Clip source audio gains 0..1, silence if absent. One music track has source-in and nonnegative timeline-start, gain 0..1; summed source+music does not normalize or duck. Users must lower gains to avoid clipping. Duration is the sum of clip frame counts; extra audio is cut to this end.
Captions use Segoe UI, #ddd at rounded(canvasWidth*.045), centered at 84% canvas height, translucent black box, [start,end) timing. Explicit line breaks supported; overlaps rejected. Preview is source-decoder-based and will pause/switch at shot boundaries; the rendered output is continuous. HTML scaling/rasterization and compression differ slightly; this is not a frame-exact effects preview. Unsupported Electron codecs show a readable error: use H264 MP4/AAC for live source preview; no proxy generation claimed. Mixed-rate/aspect rendering and exact cut frames need proof. Custom motion curves/effects remain out of v1.
Source identity: path+size+mtime_ns plus full SHA256 recorded on import, SHA256 verified before/after each clip encode and music final. Export freezes revision and identity; changed input fails visibly. Files remain user-owned, not copied or moved. Import hashing can take time on large episode files.
Resource admission: assistant Qwen/Kokoro jobs and editor render share one in-process resource_gate; export also obtains existing media execution.lock. The old Neural has its own unchanged scheduler. Real-time dictation is separate and not part of this assistant gate; export uses low priority, two CPU codec threads and one filter thread. This avoids expensive assistant/render overlap in Neural2; it is not a machine-wide resource guarantee. Export queue <=4, one per project, interrupted exports reconciled on restart without replay. Native IPC accepts only own main frame and registered successful export IDs.
