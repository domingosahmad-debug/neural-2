# TonyFX 0.60 narrative-accurate exact-replica contract

## Analyzer V6

Deep Analyze retains the frame-native transition, curve, repeat, and certificate rules from 0.59. It additionally emits a deterministic narrative energy map:

- every shot is assigned a measured phase and editorial function;
- visual and audio energy remain separate evidence values;
- first-highlight and climax shots receive explicit peak priority;
- peak slots define a minimum candidate-motion requirement;
- dialogue-active shots require speaker-coherent visuals, while non-dialogue shots reject unrelated visible mouth movement as an automatic choice.

The footage index records the actual sample time plus motion, optical flow, pixel change, luma, contrast, subject area, and shot scale. Search combines those measurements with OpenCLIP/DINO similarity.

## Builder

Exact mode must use the winning source moment inside the selected layer. Choosing the correct layer but defaulting to its in-point is a contract failure.

Before AE mutation, the Builder refuses:

- a source layer without an executable vision-matched window;
- a matched native-speed range shorter than the target shot;
- calm footage assigned to a certified first-highlight or climax slot;
- a repeated or reversed motif that does not reuse the measured source window;
- the existing 0.59 transition, curve, provider, repeat, speed, and ambiguity failures.

Each built scene stores source time, match confidence, motion score, narrative phase, and editorial function in its assignment record and layer comment. First-highlight and climax frames receive named composition markers.

## Comparison

Finished-edit comparison keeps independent format, cut, transition, curve, duration, framing, and repeat-topology gates. Version 4 adds a separate peak-energy gate. The edit fails when a reference highlight or climax is filled with footage whose visual energy is below the required absolute and within-edit percentile thresholds.
