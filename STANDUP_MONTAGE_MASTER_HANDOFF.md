## Transitions

- Transitions belong between two clips, not randomly in the middle of a shot.
- Approved families:
  - Directional blur/smear
  - Fast horizontal or vertical whip/slide
  - BlurMoCurves zoom blur
  - Brief glow pulse or exposure flash
  - Position impact shake
  - Opacity blend/match dissolve
  - Short black dip
  - Flash plus shake
  - RGB/chromatic hit when stylistically appropriate
  - Optical ripple or glimmer only when it looks polished and reference-supported
- Slides must be fast, graph-driven, and carried cleanly into the next shot. Avoid sluggish travel.
- Directional blur should briefly cover the motion and return to zero quickly.
- Impact shake should be position-based, clearly felt, and settle exactly to center.
- Glow supports impact but should not bleach faces unless the reference does.
- Transitions must be adjusted for shot duration; do not blindly apply the same number of frames everywhere.
